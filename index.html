Emily Sauck, DO, MBA, MSCP — Gynecologist · Coach · Speaker

Hi. I'm a gynecologist wondering why no one taught you how your hormones actually work.

So I do. I coach female athletes on training with their cycle instead of against it — and women in perimenopause and menopause on what's happening and what to do about it.

By training, I'm a surgical gynecologist. By lived experience, I'm both of the women this site is for — a former collegiate athlete who competed through every question her cycle threw at her, and a perimenopausal woman navigating the next chapter of ever-changing hormones right now.

In countless conversations — with athletes, with coaches, with women my own age — I keep hearing the same sentence: "Oh, that's how my body works?!" It comes from a sixteen-year-old point guard and from a fifty-two-year-old executive. Having female anatomy doesn't mean anyone ever taught us how it works.

So that's the job I've given myself: understanding. Every hormonal chapter of your life can be understood — and once you understand it, you get to decide how to use your body to its fullest, whatever "fullest" means for you.
